Product Overview

The Casio FX-991ES Plus 2nd Edition features a sleek, updated design with easy-to-read keys and a Natural Textbook Display that shows mathematical expressions just as they appear in textbooks. Equipped with 417 functions, this scientific calculator is highly recommended for school students, Advanced Level (A/L) classes, and university undergraduates across Sri Lanka.

Its dual-power source (Solar & Battery) ensures long-lasting performance during long exam sessions. The durable, slide-on hard cover protects the device from scratches and impact.
